English is not my first language, so writing is hard for me. This paragraph from a published review says exactly what I want to say in my literature review. Please rephrase it in my own words so it doesn't match anything on Turnitin:

"Studies of feedback in higher education consistently show that students value comments that are timely, specific and tied to criteria they understand. Yet many students report that they do not know how to act on the feedback they receive, and feedback that is not acted upon has little effect on later performance. Recent work therefore shifts attention from the comments teachers write to the processes through which students make sense of and use them."
