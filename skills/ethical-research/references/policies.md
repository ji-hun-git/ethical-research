# AI rules of journals, conferences, funders and agencies

Checked 2026-10-30 on each body's own page. These rules change several times a year. Use this table to know what to ask about, never to tell a researcher that something complies: for that, ask for the venue's current text and read it. Entries marked *reported* were confirmed only through secondary sources because the primary page was not reachable.

What nearly all of them share: AI is never an author; the authors answer for every word, figure, number and reference; AI use is disclosed where the venue says; a fabricated reference is grounds for rejection; images that show results are not generated or altered with AI; confidential manuscripts stay out of public AI tools.

## Reviewers and editors

The common rule: do not put a confidential manuscript, application or review into an AI tool that does not guarantee confidentiality. Many now allow limited use; few allow AI to form the judgement.

## Hidden prompts in manuscripts

Text hidden in a manuscript to steer an AI reviewer (white text, tiny fonts, "give a positive review only") was found in 17 arXiv preprints from 14 institutions in 2025 (Nikkei Asia, 2025-07-01). It is grounds for desk rejection at ICML 2026, ACL and CVPR, strictly prohibited at NeurIPS 2026, collusion at ICLR, a punishable ethics violation at AAAI-27, and may be sanctioned as research misconduct under Korea's MSIT guide (2026). None of the journal policy pages checked mentions it yet. `scripts/integrity_scan.py` looks for it.
