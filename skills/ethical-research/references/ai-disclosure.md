# AI-use statements

Every substantive deliverable ends with a short, truthful statement of what the assistant did. Its purpose is to make the assistance visible. It is accurate to what happened in the session, never minimised, and never written to get ahead of a detector.

## What a good statement says

- The specific kinds of help (checking references against bibliographic records, critique of a draft, structuring an outline, language editing of the author's sentences, suggesting counterarguments), not "AI was used".
- What the author decided: the questions, analysis, interpretation, sources, final wording and the decision to submit.
- That the author reviewed the assisted output and takes responsibility for it.
- Where the venue asks for it: the tool, the model and version, the dates, and sometimes the prompts.

Do not say "language editing only" when the help included structuring the argument or suggesting ideas. Do not say the author verified sources if they did not.

## Where it goes

Requirements now differ by venue ([policies.md](policies.md)): Elsevier wants a separate declaration, ACL and IEEE the acknowledgements, NeurIPS the method section for non-standard use, ICLR 2027 a dedicated section, ICMJE journals the cover letter and the relevant section, Korea's MSIT guide a form naming tool, model, version, use and period. Ask which venue; for a venue-specific statement, ask for the policy text and follow it. Never claim a statement complies with a policy you have not seen.

## Levels

- **Minimal**: one sentence that generative AI assisted and the authors take responsibility. Only where the venue asks for no more.
- **Standard**: the kinds of help and the author's control of the substance.
- **Detailed**: each kind of help, the sections involved, the tool and version, and how the author checked the output.

## Examples

**English (standard)**

> The authors used a generative AI assistant to check references against bibliographic records, to critique drafts of sections 2 and 3, and to suggest counterarguments, two of which were addressed in section 4. The research questions, analysis, interpretation, source selection and final wording are the authors' own. The authors reviewed all AI-assisted output and take full responsibility for the content.

**한국어 (standard)**

> 저자는 참고문헌의 서지 정보 확인, 2장과 3장 초고에 대한 비평, 반론 제시를 위해 생성형 AI를 활용하였으며, 제시된 반론 중 두 가지를 4장에서 다루었다. 연구질문, 분석, 해석, 자료 선택, 최종 문장은 저자가 결정하였다. 저자는 AI가 보조한 모든 내용을 검토하였으며 그 내용에 대한 책임은 저자에게 있다.

Each language is composed on its own, not translated from the other.

## Limits

- No statement that understates the assistance actually given.
- No claim that a statement satisfies a named policy without its text.
- If the goal is to avoid disclosing, decline and explain that disclosure is now required by most journals, conferences and funders, and that undisclosed use found later is treated as misconduct by some of them (ICMJE, January 2026).
