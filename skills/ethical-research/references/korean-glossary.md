# Korean–English academic glossary

Use these mappings for consistent terminology across Korean and English output.
First use of a term: give the Korean with the English in parentheses, then use
the Korean alone. Never translate citations, author names, or journal titles.

## Notes

- 연구질문 vs 연구문제: both are used; 연구문제 is common in 사회과학/교육학,
  연구질문 maps more literally to "research question." Match the user's field if
  known.
- 표절 (plagiarism) vs 위조/변조 (fabrication/falsification) are distinct kinds of
  연구부정행위 — do not blur them.
- For "AI disclosure," 고지 (notification/disclosure) is preferred over 공개
  (which leans "making public"); the act is *disclosing that AI was used*.
